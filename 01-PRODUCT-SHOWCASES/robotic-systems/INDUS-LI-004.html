Industrial Collaborative Robots: Power & Force Limiting to ISO/TS 15066
[INDUS-LI-004] • Product Showcases • Visual Asset Fallback Verified

Collaborative robots shouldn't force you to choose between sluggish 5 kg payloads and bulky safety fencing.

Many plants need collaborative operation alongside human operators without sacrificing industrial payload capacity.

The INDUS Cobot Series features dual-channel torque sensors at every joint with 1 ms safety monitoring loops. Compliant with ISO 10218-1 and ISO/TS 15066, it operates with force-limiting safety during collaborative tasks and accelerates to 2.5 m/s when area scanners confirm the perimeter is clear.

This hybrid speed-and-separation architecture allows compact cell footprints while maintaining production cycle times.
