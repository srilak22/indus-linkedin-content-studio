Here is how precision motion engineering resolves industrial manufacturing bottlenecks: Maximizing Factory Floor Density.

The Problem: A high-density consumer electronics factory needed to double assembly capacity but had zero available square footage in their cleanroom.

Technical Explanation: Floor-mounted robotic pedestals consume valuable square footage and restrict operator walking aisles.

The INDUS Solution: We deployed ceiling-suspended inverted articulated cobots and compact overhead linear gantries that feed components from above.

Business Value: Production throughput doubled within the exact same factory footprint, avoiding a multimillion-dollar building expansion.

Key Technical Specifications
  • Floor Space Saved: 100% Zero Additional Floor Area Consumed
  • Production Throughput: Doubled Output in Existing Cleanroom
  • Mounting Options: Inverted Ceiling, Wall-Angle, High-Gantry
  • Cable Management: Internal Dress Pack via Hollow-Through Bores
  • Facility Savings: Avoided Estimated $2.4M Building Expansion
