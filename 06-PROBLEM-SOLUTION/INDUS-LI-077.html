Unexpected main bearing or gearbox failures on transfer presses can halt an entire production facility.

Sub-surface fatigue creates microscopic micro-spalling on bearing races that escalates into seizure under heavy cyclic loads.

Retrofitting robotic axes with high-frequency piezoelectric accelerometers and continuous Motor Current Signature Analysis (MCSA) captures defect frequencies early. Characteristic bearing peaks trigger maintenance alerts weeks before audible noise begins.

Maintenance teams schedule component replacements during planned weekend turnarounds rather than facing emergency line halts.

Key Technical Specifications
  • Monitoring Modality: High-frequency vibration FFT + Phase Current MCSA
  • Fault Frequency Tracking: Monitors BPFO, BPFI, and cage fault peaks
  • Lead Time Window: Multi-week advance detection of sub-surface wear
  • Maintenance Shift: Transitions cell maintenance from reactive to planned
  • Connectivity: Direct MQTT / OPC UA alerts to plant CMMS
