AMR vs AGV: Navigating Fixed Guidepaths vs Autonomous LiDAR SLAM Mapping
[INDUS-LI-068] • Educational & Technical Content • Visual Asset Fallback Verified

People often use AGV and AMR interchangeably. Their underlying navigation principles are fundamentally different.

Choosing the wrong mobility platform can leave you with rigid tracks or unnecessary software complexity.

Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs) follow fixed physical paths: magnetic floor tape, embedded guide wires, or floor QR markers. If an obstacle blocks the path, the AGV halts. Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) use LiDAR SLAM to perceive obstacles and dynamically steer around them.

AGVs suit fixed, predictable production loops; AMRs excel in dynamic warehouses where routes change frequently.
