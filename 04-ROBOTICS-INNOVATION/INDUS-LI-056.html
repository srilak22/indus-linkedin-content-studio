How is technological innovation reshaping modern precision motion? Let's break down 3D Vision-Guided Random Part Picking.

Picking shiny, randomly jumbled automotive parts from deep wire bins without robot gripper collisions has long been a key automation hurdle.

Combining high-contrast structured light 3D sensors with GPU-accelerated motion planning algorithms, the INDUS Bin-Picking Suite captures dense point clouds, calculates optimal part grasp orientations, and generates 6-axis collision-free trajectories avoiding bin walls in under 120 milliseconds.

Key Technical Specifications
  • Cycle Planning Time: < 120 ms Total (Capture to Trajectory)
  • Point Cloud Resolution: 2.3 Million 3D Coordinates
  • Bin Collision Avoidance: Dynamic Mesh Obstacle Checking
  • Grip Orientation: 6-DOF Arbitrary Surface Grasp Pose
  • Pick Success Rate: > 99.2% on Irregular Stamped Metal Parts
